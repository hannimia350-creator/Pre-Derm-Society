About Us

The Pre-Derm Society is a student-led organization designed to connect students interested in dermatology and healthcare. Through education, discussion, events, and hands-on opportunities, we aim to help students explore the field and learn more about careers in medicine.

What We Do

🩺 Education

Learn about dermatology, medical careers, and the path toward becoming a physician.

🔬 Research

Explore current topics in dermatology and discover how research contributes to patient care.

🤝 Community

Connect with other students who are interested in medicine and dermatology.
