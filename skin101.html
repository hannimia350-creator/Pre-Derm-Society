Skin Science 01 · Foundations

The science
of skin.

Before exploring dermatology, it helps to understand the organ dermatologists study every day. Let's start with the fundamentals.

01 · The basics

What is skin?

Skin is the body's largest organ. It creates a protective boundary between the body and its environment while also contributing to sensation and temperature regulation.

02 · Structure

Three major layers.

Skin is organized into layers, each with different structures and functions.

01 · Epidermis The outermost layer and an important part of the body's protective barrier.
02 · Dermis Contains structures including nerves, blood vessels, glands, and hair follicles.
03 · Subcutaneous tissue Deeper tissue containing connective tissue and fat that helps cushion and insulate.
03 · Function

More than a barrier.

Skin helps protect the body, allows us to sense our surroundings, and plays an important role in maintaining body temperature. It is constantly interacting with the world around us.
