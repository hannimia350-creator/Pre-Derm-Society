SKIN 101 🧬

Welcome! In this mini lesson, you'll learn the basic structure and functions of skin, then test what you've learned.

01 — What is skin?

Skin is the body's largest organ. It forms a protective barrier between your body and the outside environment. It also helps with sensation, temperature regulation, and protection.

02 — The three main layers

Epidermis: The outermost layer of skin. It provides much of the body's protective barrier.


Dermis: The layer underneath the epidermis. It contains structures such as blood vessels, nerves, hair follicles, and glands.


Subcutaneous tissue: The deeper layer beneath the dermis that contains connective tissue and fat and helps cushion and insulate the body.

03 — Why does skin matter?

Skin is constantly interacting with the environment. It helps protect the body, allows us to sense our surroundings, and contributes to maintaining a stable internal temperature.
