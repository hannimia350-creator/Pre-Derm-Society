02 — Skin science

Your skin is an organ.

Your skin is living tissue with important jobs that help protect your body and interact with the environment around you.

Skin health is about understanding how skin works — not about looking “perfect.”

03 — The basics

Meet the three main layers.

EPIDERMIS The outermost layer of skin.
DERMIS Contains structures including blood vessels, nerves, hair follicles and glands.
SUBCUTANEOUS LAYER A deeper layer containing connective tissue and fat.
04 — Quick challenge

05 — Skin barrier

What is the skin barrier?

The outer part of the skin helps form a protective barrier between your body and the outside environment.

It also helps limit water loss.

Think: protect + maintain + defend.

06 — Think critically

Science or social media?

“This skincare trick works for EVERYONE.”

07 — Digital literacy

How do we check a claim?

WHO? Who made the claim?
EVIDENCE? What evidence supports it?
MOTIVE? Are they trying to sell something?
VERIFY? Can you check it with a trustworthy source?
